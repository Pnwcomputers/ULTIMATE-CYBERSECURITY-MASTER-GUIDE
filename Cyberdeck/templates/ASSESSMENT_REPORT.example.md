## Evidence and Delivery

Record original and derivative evidence references, redactions, manifest location, transfer method, recipient verification and access restrictions. Do not embed secrets, unnecessary personal data or full packet captures in the report. Hashes detect byte changes relative to a protected baseline; they do not authenticate acquisition or establish custody independently.

## Closeout and Retest

Record equipment removal, temporary access revocation, outstanding issues, retest scope, recipient acceptance and agreed retention/deletion actions. Close each finding only after the defined retest and independent review, or document accepted residual risk with the responsible owner.
