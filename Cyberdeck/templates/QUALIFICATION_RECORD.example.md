For power qualification also record input voltage, measurement point, idle/load draw, test duration, battery energy rating, usable runtime, temperature, connected devices and shutdown behavior. Estimate runtime from usable watt-hours divided by measured watts, then confirm by measurement; conversion losses and peak loads prevent nameplate capacity from being a runtime guarantee.
